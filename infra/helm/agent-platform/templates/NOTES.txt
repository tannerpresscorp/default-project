This chart is gated and renders nothing until `global.enabled=true` plus at
least one component is enabled. It is an unvalidated starting point, not a
production deployment.

Required when enabled:

- `global.image.repository` and `global.image.tag` (prefer immutable digests)
- `api.existingSecret` and/or `worker.existingSecret`
- database and Temporal CIDRs for egress policy
- `networkPolicy.ingressNamespace` for API ingress

Run `helm template` and a policy review before applying to any cluster.
